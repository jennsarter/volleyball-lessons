03 / Your Coach

WHY I
COACH.

Volleyball teaches life lessons beyond the court—confidence, communication and discipline, to name a few. In private lessons, I build these alongside technical skills for a complete package.

Confidence is the foundation of what I teach. It comes from learning skills correctly, positive reinforcement and steady improvement that leaves players feeling prepared for game day. I teach the precise mechanics of a skill and the "why" behind it, meeting players where they are while pushing them toward their potential.

I love seeing a player's face light up when a skill finally "clicks"—like landing that first overhand serve over the net. That's why I coach.

— COACH JENN

COLLEGIATE VOLLEYBALL EXPERIENCE

Hailing from Champaign, Ill., Jenn Sarter played NCAA Division I volleyball for Mississippi State University (MSU) in the SEC, with a full-ride scholarship. As a 6’0” middle blocker, Jenn holds MSU’s all-time individual match records for total blocks and block assists against the LSU Tigers. She also holds records for attack percentage and was an AVCA Academic All-American.
